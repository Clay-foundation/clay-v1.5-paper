# E8 — Reviewer-requested controls (frozen 2026-11-05 22:25 UTC, before any E8 score)

Follows `../PROTOCOL-COMMON.md` (Clay v1.5 via `common/clayrt.py`, lat/lon in **training order** for every metadata-ON arm, fixed heads `evalkit.clf_head` / `evalkit.reg_head`, 100 km `evalkit.hblock` blocks, 1,000 block-bootstrap resamples with seed 1729, paired contrasts share resamples). Motivation: mock peer review of 2026-11-05. No E8 score had been computed when this file was written. Prior-experiment scores (E1–E4) were known; that is why every E8 endpoint is a pre-declared contrast with a pre-declared reading.

## Item 1 — E3 context-matched cross-sensor control (blocking)

**Locations.** All 6,592 E3 locations (`E3_cross_sensor/manifest.csv`), same labels (WorldCover 2021 majority of the central 320 m cell), same 7 classes {10,20,30,40,50,60,80}, same 100 km blocks and split (`hblock` → `block_split`; train = train ∪ val). We attempt every location (no subsampling for the main arms). The evaluation set **L** = locations with a valid S2-256 and a valid NAIP-256 chip, in the 7 classes. Every comparison to E3 32 px numbers is recomputed **on L** from the cached E3 embeddings (training-order meta-ON from the E3 amendment; meta-OFF and DINOv2 from E3 `emb.npz`).

**Chips (new).** Footprint = 2.56 × 2.56 km centred on the E3 cell centre, in the E3 cell's UTM CRS on the same 10 m grid (the central 32 × 32 px of the new S2 chip is the E3 S2 chip footprint).
- **S2-256.** The exact E3 S2 items (same day, `s2_ids` in the E3 manifest; mosaic by solar day as E3), 10 Clay bands, raw values, 256 × 256 at 10 m, nearest. Valid if all 10 bands are > 0 at every pixel and ≥ 90 % of SCL ∈ {4,5,6,7}. No other day is tried (keeps the acquisition identical to E3). Sanity check reported: fraction of locations whose central 32 × 32 crop equals the cached E3 chip exactly.
- **NAIP-256 @ 10 m.** NAIP R,G,B,NIR over the same 2.56 km footprint, read with GDAL average resampling (overviews allowed) to 256 × 256 → 10 m; embedded with **gsd = 10**, acquisition date = E3 NAIP date. Source = the E3 NAIP item; pixels that are nodata there are filled from other `naip` items of the same year intersecting the footprint (closest date first). Valid if < 1 % of pixels are all-zero.
- **NAIP-native pooled (secondary, "if cheap").** Same footprint at 1.25 m (2048 × 2048, GDAL average, as E3's NAIP), cut into 8 × 8 tiles of 256 px (320 m), each embedded at gsd = 1.25 with the chip-centre lat/lon and NAIP date; readout = mean over the 64 tiles of the CLS (and of the mean token). Run on locations in the order of sha256(`claypaper-e8|loc_id`), target the first 2,000, hard GPU-time cap 3 h; whatever prefix completes is reported, with its size.

**Features.** Clay CLS meta-ON (primary), Clay mean-patch meta-ON, Clay **center** readout (mean of the central 4 × 4 patch tokens = the labelled 320 m cell; secondary), Clay CLS meta-OFF (secondary), DINOv2-L/14-reg4 CLS on display RGB at 224 px (`to_display_rgb`), for S2-256 and NAIP-256@10m (reference).

**Arms (S2→NAIP, as frozen in E3 `evaluate.py`).** native-full NAIP head; native-full S2 head (on S2 test); zero-shot (S2 head incl. its scaler, applied to NAIP); zero-shot after re-standardising NAIP; Procrustes n ∈ {100, 1000} (5 seeds, `evalkit.n_budget`); majority class. Metrics: macro-F1 (primary), accuracy. Retrieval: each S2 query against the NAIP gallery of all L locations, cosine, raw and per-sensor-centred; R@1, R@10, median rank; CIs from 1,000 query-block resamples. Secondary retrieval variant: gallery excludes the other cells of the same E3 tile (their 2.56 km footprints overlap the query's). Cosine descriptives: mean paired S2–NAIP cosine, mean unpaired (random permutation, seed 0) cross-sensor cosine, within-S2 random-pair cosine; CI on paired − unpaired via location-block bootstrap.

**Same comparisons for E3 32 px on L.** S2-32 (10 m) → NAIP-320 m (1.25 m, gsd 1.25), all the arms above, same seeds and resamples.

**Primary endpoint 1a.** Δ256 = macro-F1(zero-shot S2-256 → NAIP-256@10m) − macro-F1(native NAIP-256@10m), Clay CLS meta-ON; and the paired contrast Δ256 − Δ32 (Δ32 = the E3 primary Δ recomputed on L), shared test-block resamples.
Pre-declared reading: |Δ256| < 0.10 → E3's failure was a chip-context artefact (transfer works when context and GSD match). Δ256 − Δ32 CI above 0 but |Δ256| ≥ 0.10 → context explains part of the gap. CI of Δ256 − Δ32 including 0 → context does not explain the gap.
Secondary: zero-shot macro-F1 256 vs 32 (paired), Procrustes-1000, R@10 (paired query-block contrast), paired−unpaired cosine, all three features, DINOv2, NAIP-native pooled.

**Item 1b — same-sensor, different chip size.** Heads trained on S2-32 CLS applied unchanged to S2-256 CLS of the same locations, and vice versa; also NAIP-320m@1.25 → NAIP-256@10m and vice versa (differs in context and GSD). Same arm set (native, zero-shot, re-standardised, Procrustes).
**Primary endpoint 1b.** Δctx = macro-F1(S2-32 head → S2-256) − macro-F1(native S2-256 head), Clay CLS meta-ON. Reading: if |Δctx| is comparable to |Δ32|, chip-size/context offset alone breaks a head as much as a sensor change; if |Δctx| < 0.10, the S2 token space is stable across context and the E3 offset is a sensor offset.

## Item 3 — E1 sensitivity (CPU, cached E1 shards on host `E1_label_efficiency/aef_out_v2`)
(a) **Re-split.** Per task, pool the already-embedded rows (train rows with a chip ∪ tier-0 test rows with a chip; change tasks need both chips), assign `hblock` 100 km → `block_split`; train = train ∪ val, test = test blocks (no test cap). n = 10 labels/class (`label_budget`, strata = label, or AEF partition for regression, 5 seeds), linear heads, clay_cls vs stats, same metrics (BA / R²), paired block bootstrap. Compare with the AEF-split clay_cls − stats at n = 10 from E1 `results.json`. Tasks whose re-split yields < 2 test blocks or a class missing in train are reported as skipped.
(b) **Train–test distance.** For each AEF test row (tier-0, with chip), great-circle distance to the nearest train row with a chip of the same task: median, IQR, fraction < 1 km, < 10 km, < 100 km. Also for the re-split as a reference.
(c) **Tabulation** from E1 `results.json` (`aef_suite`): clay_cls, clay_mean, clay_center, coords (linear head) per task at n = 10 and max, with CIs; realised max n per class (n_train_mean / n_classes) and the fraction of AEF train rows available (n_train / n_train_rows_full); LCMAP land-use change (lcmap_luc) stats at max vs chance 1/n_classes (CI above chance?). No new scores in (c).

## Item 4 — E2 location probe (cached E2 embeddings on host `E2_tokens/emb`)
Using the E2 ridge probe exactly (`reg_head` on CLS → lat and → lon, fit on all train chips, predicted on test chips), report for each sensor (S2 primary) and arms CLS meta-ON training order, CLS meta-ON documented order, CLS meta-OFF: longitude R², latitude R², median great-circle error (km) of (pred lat, pred lon); overall and for test chips inside vs outside the CONUS box (lon −124.7…−66.9, lat 24.5…49.4) with block-bootstrap CIs over test blocks within each subset; paired ON − OFF contrasts. Secondary probe: ridge to the 3-D unit vector (x, y, z) → great-circle error (avoids longitude wrap). R² within a subset uses the subset's own variance (it is expected to be lower in CONUS by construction).
