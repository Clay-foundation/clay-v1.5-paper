What changed relative to the 2026-11-06 table (issue #16):
- The old producer was not in the repository, filtered each arm independently (Clay and TESSERA pixel had different test rows on 14/15 tasks, e.g. GLanCE 550 vs 494) and picked point-feature years by earliest / latest / nearest year. The new producer joins AEF / TESSERA to the row's label year(s) by exact (x, y, year), and scores every comparison on a set of arms with identical test rows and identical training draws, with paired 100 km block-bootstrap intervals shared by all arms of the set. The "common" set (all chip arms plus `aef_mean128`, `aef_ov32_px`, `tessera_pixel`) feeds Table 2 Panel C: its test sizes are listed in section 0b of the summary (e.g. GLanCE 494, ASTER 456, Canada fine 1,125, LUCAS LC 1,996).
- Label years come from the sampled point-years (`build_label_years.py`, "points" mode): 33,190 rows take their single sampled year, 1,085 change rows their unique consecutive pair, 42 change rows a chip-year tie-break; 34,413 rows have no sampled point and drop out. The exact mode (AEF `evaluation.zip` valid periods) was not run: the archive was not on the compute host.
- Budgets. Each cell records the nominal draw (from all tier-filtered train rows) and the achieved draw (from the set's rows). Headline counts use cells whose achieved training size equals the nominal size on every seed; "strict E1-complete" (every nominally selected row present) is stored as `budget_complete` and shown separately. At 10 labels per class all 15 tasks run at full size on the chip set. At max most cells in the common set are NOT at the nominal max: LUCAS LC trains on 1,861 rows (nominal 12,000), LUCAS LU 750 (4,500), US trees 1,819 (11,700), GLanCE 2,977 (3,300), ASTER 1,853 (2,000). Panel C "max" values for those tasks are achieved-budget values and must be labelled as such. Only Africa crop mask and Ethiopia crops are strict-complete at both budgets in the common set.
- Each scored set stores SHA-256 digests of its test ids, train pool and per-seed selected training ids (summary section 0 checks that all arms of every one of the 103 sets carry the same digests and n_test).
- Heads: linear only (kNN is in `e5_sota_comparators.json`).

The sections below are the 2026-11-06 text, kept for the record. Where they conflict with this update (the matched sets, the OlmoEarth / Prithvi arms, which were run after that text was written, and the "max" numbers), this update wins.


**What was measured.** E1's AEF-suite rows, splits, label draws, test caps (≤ 50 per class, 500 for regression), heads (logistic C = 1 / RidgeCV), budgets (10 labels per class and max) and 100 km block bootstrap (1,000 resamples) were reused unchanged. Clay CLS (primary) and Clay centre-token (2 × 2 central patches) are E1's stored vectors. Each comparator is paired with Clay on exactly the rows where it has a feature. Clay CLS on the full AEF set reproduces E1 to ≤ 0.001 on every cell except GLanCE max (+0.005).

Comparators, all read from public releases for the calendar year of each label:
- **AEF annual embeddings** (Source Cooperative mirror), in three readouts:
  - 1.28 km mean (primary, the same footprint as the Clay chip);
  - 320 m overview pixel;
  - 10 m pixel at the label point (AEF's own protocol; a subset only).
- **TESSERA v1.1** (zarr store): 10 m pixel (primary) and 1.28 km patch mean.
- **Coordinates-only control**: real spherical harmonics of lat/lon to degree 10.

The protocol was frozen before any score, with three dated amendments.

**What the contrasts show.**
- **Clay loses where the target is crop type.** At n = 10, Clay CLS minus AEF mean is −0.140 balanced accuracy (BA) [−0.171, −0.106] on Canada coarse crops (Clay 0.260, AEF 0.400). It is also negative on Canada fine crops, GLanCE, LUCAS LC and US trees. Clay CLS minus TESSERA pixel is −0.200 [−0.212, −0.187] on LUCAS LC. These are targets where a full year of observations helps, and Clay sees one date.
- **Clay wins where a single acquisition matches the label.**
  - OpenET (monthly evapotranspiration): Clay minus AEF is +0.540 R² [0.424, 0.664]. Annual embeddings cannot resolve a month: AEF R² is −0.44, TESSERA R² is −0.44, coordinates R² is −0.63.
  - Africa crop mask: +0.120 BA [0.058, 0.180].
- **Most other tasks are ties.** This includes ASTER emissivity, Ethiopia crops, oil palm (Descals), LCMAP land cover/use and LCMAP land-use change. LCMAP land-cover change is a loss against AEF, −0.066 [−0.120, −0.014].
- **AEF 10 m pixel** (only 4 tasks have complete n = 10 draws): Clay ties on Africa, Descals and Ethiopia. It loses on LCMAP land use, −0.196 [−0.231, −0.162]. There the AEF pixel (0.795) is far above the AEF 1.28 km mean (0.621), so point-support matters for plot labels.
- **Clay is not a place proxy.** Clay beats the coordinates-only control on 10 of 13 tasks. The one loss is US trees (−0.040 [−0.050, −0.030]).

**Caveats.**
- **Different observation windows.** Clay sees one Sentinel-2 date. AEF and TESSERA summarise a full year of multi-sensor data. Their annual product is not the per-period AEF embedding used in the AEF paper.
- **Partial AEF pixel arm.** Reading a 10 m pixel costs one 1024² tile per band. The source failed twice in bursts (about 20k reads errored). The 10 m arm therefore covers 8,868 point-years and is complete for the n = 10 draws on 4 tasks; the rest are shown grey as provisional.
- **Few max cells for TESSERA.** E1's completeness rule removes a task's max cell if any row it needs is missing. 1–6% of rows have no TESSERA embedding, so only 2 tasks have max for TESSERA, and 8 have max for AEF.
- **Possible label exposure for the comparators.** The AEF suite is AEF's own development benchmark, and AEF's training corpus is undisclosed. TESSERA v2 (not used here) selected models on this suite; whether v1.1 did is not established. Clay used no labels, but spatial overlap of these rows with Clay's training chips was not checked.
- **Arms not run.** SatCLIP, Clay ⊕ SatCLIP and DOFA with its own normalisation were not run. (OlmoEarth v1.3 and Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M were embedded after this text was written; they appear only in the 2026-11-17 update, with centre and mean readouts and no CLS arm.)

Embedding size per vector: Clay 1024-D (2 KB fp16); AEF 64-D (64 B int8); TESSERA 128-D (132 B). Test-row coverage against AEF's full released test sets is in `e5_coverage.csv`; the capped sets are 0.3–14% of the released ones.
